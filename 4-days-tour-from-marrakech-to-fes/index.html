    Marrakech to Fes in 4 Days: Private Sahara & Cedar Forest Tour

    A relaxed 4-day private tour from Marrakech to Fes: Atlas kasbahs, a Sahara camp in Merzouga and a slow drive through the Ziz Valley, Ifrane and the Middle Atlas cedar forest.

    • 100% private tour
    • One-way: Marrakech to Fes
    • Sahara camp night
    • Free cancellation up to 14 days
    Tour overview

    Desert first, then cedar forests and Fes

    The extra day makes the difference: you split the long drive from Merzouga to Fes, sleep in the Middle Atlas and enter Fes fresh the next morning.

    Quick answer

    Four days is the comfortable way to travel from Marrakech to Fes through the Sahara. You get one night in the Dades area, one in the desert camp, one in the Middle Atlas and a morning in the cedar forests of Azrou before reaching Fes.

    1. Marrakech
    2. Ait Ben Haddou
    3. Dades Valley
    4. Merzouga
    5. Midelt
    6. Ifrane
    7. Fes
    Day by day

    4-day itinerary step by step

    1

    Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou – Ouarzazate – Dades Valley≈ 330 km · 8 h with stops

    Cross the Tizi n’Tichka pass, visit Ait Ben Haddou and continue through Ouarzazate and the Skoura palm grove to the Dades Valley.

    • Night: hotel in the Dades Valley
    • Dinner included
    2

    Dades – Todra Gorge – Merzouga – camel trek≈ 300 km · 6–7 h driving

    Walk through Todra Gorge, drive to Merzouga and ride a camel into the dunes at sunset. Dinner and night in a desert camp.

    • Night: desert camp in Merzouga
    • Camel trek included
    • Dinner and breakfast included
    3

    Merzouga – Ziz Valley – Midelt – Ifrane≈ 400 km · 7–8 h driving

    Sunrise in the dunes, then drive north through the Ziz Valley and Midelt, cross the Middle Atlas and arrive in the alpine-style town of Ifrane.

    • Night: hotel in Ifrane or Azrou
    • Breakfast and dinner included
    4

    Ifrane – Azrou cedar forest – Fes≈ 80 km · 2 h driving

    Walk in the cedar forest of Azrou, where wild Barbary macaques live, and drive on to Fes. Arrive around midday with time to settle in.

    • End: your hotel in Fes
    • Breakfast included

    FAQ

    Questions about the 4 days tour from marrakech to fes

    Why choose 4 days instead of 3 from Marrakech to Fes?

    The last drive from Merzouga to Fes is about 8 to 9 hours. Splitting it with a night in the Middle Atlas gives you a lighter final day and time for the cedar forest.

    Will I see wild monkeys in Azrou?

    Often, yes. Barbary macaques live in the cedar forest near Azrou, but they are wild animals, so sightings depend on the day. Please do not feed them.

    Can we add a guided visit of Fes?

    Yes. We can book a licensed local guide for the medina on the afternoon you arrive or the next morning, for an additional fee.

    How do I book the tour?

    Send us your dates by WhatsApp or the contact form. You confirm with a 20% deposit and pay the rest in Morocco. Cancellation is free up to 14 days before departure.
