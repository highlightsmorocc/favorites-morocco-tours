    2 Weeks in Morocco: A 14-Day Itinerary

    Two weeks is enough to see Morocco's north, imperial cities, desert and coast. This itinerary shows one way to do it.

    Favorites Morocco Tours · Updated: 27 November 2026

    Quick answer

    With two weeks in Morocco you can see the blue town of Chefchaouen, the old medina of Fes, a night in the Sahara, the kasbahs of the south and Marrakech. A common route runs from the north to the south: Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes, the Sahara, the Dades Valley, Marrakech and the coast. Plan one day per stop, with a few longer drives.

    The 14-day route

    • Days 1–2Tangier and ChefchaouenHarbour city, then the blue town
    • Days 3–4FesThe old medina and its crafts
    • Days 5–6The SaharaDunes, camels and a desert camp
    • Days 7–8Dades and Aït BenhaddouGorges, kasbahs and a UNESCO ksar
    • Days 9–11MarrakechSouks, gardens and palaces
    • Days 12–13EssaouiraA relaxed coastal stop
    • Day 14CasablancaThe Hassan II Mosque and your flight

    Long drives. Some days include several hours on the road, so plan rest time and travel with a private driver.

    Planning tips

    • Choose spring or autumn for the best weather
    • Keep a flexible day in your plan
    • Fly into one city and out of another
    • Book desert camps and riads early
    • Share your interests so we can adjust the route

    2 weeks in Morocco: frequently asked questions

    Is two weeks enough for Morocco?

    Yes, it covers the north, the imperial cities, the desert and the coast at a comfortable pace.

    What is the best route for two weeks?

    A north-to-south route from Tangier to Marrakech, with the Sahara in the middle, is popular.

    How many nights in the desert?

    One night is usual, and two lets you relax more.

    Do I need a private driver?

    It is not required, but it saves time and makes long distances easier.

    When is the best time to go?

    Spring and autumn are popular for mild weather.
