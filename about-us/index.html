    About Favorites Morocco Tours

    A Local Moroccan Team Crafting Your Private Morocco Tours

    We are Favorites Morocco Tours, a private tour company run by local Moroccan driver-guides. We design tailor-made journeys from Marrakech, Fes, Casablanca and Tangier to the Sahara — with your own driver, handpicked riads and desert camps, and real people answering on WhatsApp 24/7.

    Our story

    Born in Morocco, built around you

    Favorites Morocco Tours started with a simple idea: travellers deserve to see Morocco the way locals live it — not from the window of a crowded bus. Our team grew up between the Atlas Mountains, the imperial cities and the Sahara, and we know every road, kasbah and oasis along the way.

    Today we plan private journeys for couples, families and small groups from all over the world, especially from Spain, Mexico, Latin America, the UK and the USA. Every itinerary is built from scratch around your dates, pace and budget, and you travel only with your own driver-guide.

    “We don't sell tours from a catalogue. We plan the trip we would plan for our own family — and we stay with you until the last day.”— The Favorites Morocco Tours team

    FAQ

    Questions about Favorites Morocco Tours

    Who is Favorites Morocco Tours?

    Favorites Morocco Tours is a private tour company run by local Moroccan driver-guides. We organise tailor-made private tours and day trips across Morocco, from Marrakech, Fes, Casablanca, Tangier and Errachidia to the Sahara desert.

    Are your tours private or shared?

    All our tours are 100% private: only you and your group travel with your own driver-guide and vehicle, and the itinerary is adapted to your pace.

    How do I book and pay?

    Send us your dates and wishes, we prepare a personalised itinerary, and you confirm with a 20% deposit. The rest is paid in Morocco during your tour.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Cancellation is free up to 14 days before departure.

    Do your guides speak Spanish?

    Yes. Our team speaks English and Spanish, so travellers from Spain, Mexico and Latin America can plan everything in their own language.

    Ready to discover Morocco with locals?

    Tell us your dates and we'll design your private tour — free quote, no commitment.
