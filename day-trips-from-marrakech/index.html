    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best day trip from Marrakech?

    For a first visit, Ait Ben Haddou (a UNESCO kasbah) and the Ouzoud waterfalls are the most popular; for a relaxed day, choose the Ourika Valley or the Agafay desert.

    How long is a day trip from Marrakech?

    Most day trips last 8 to 11 hours, with pick-up at your hotel or riad in the morning and return in the evening.

    Are the day trips private?

    Yes, every day trip is private with your own driver; we adapt the timing and stops to your group.
