    A private full-day trip from Marrakech to the Ouzoud Waterfalls in the Middle Atlas: walk down to the 110 m falls, take a boat ride at the base and meet the Barbary macaques.

    • Free cancellation up to 14 days
    Trip overview

    Morocco’s most beautiful waterfalls, in one day

    Ouzoud is a green escape from the heat of Marrakech. The falls drop 110 m into a pool, with rainbows on sunny days, olive groves and wild monkeys on the trail.

    Quick answer

    Ouzoud is about 150 km from Marrakech, around 2.5 to 3 hours by car. The falls are at their best in spring and after rain. The trail is easy but can be slippery, so wear good shoes.

    FAQ

    Questions about the ouzoud waterfalls day trip

    When is the best time to visit Ouzoud?

    Spring (March to May) has the most water. In summer the falls are lower but the pools are good for a dip. Autumn and winter can be wet.

    Is the trail difficult?

    No, but it has many steps and can be slippery. Wear trainers or walking shoes and bring a light jacket.

    Are the monkeys safe?

    They are wild Barbary macaques. They are used to visitors, but do not feed them or carry loose food.
