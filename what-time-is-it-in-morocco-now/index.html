    What Time Is It in Morocco Now? Current Morocco Time & Time Zone Guide

    If you are wondering what time is it in Morocco now, Morocco currently uses the Africa/Casablanca time zone. Rabat, Casablanca, Marrakech, Fes, Tangier, Agadir, and other major Moroccan cities all follow the same national time.

    What Time Zone Is Morocco In?

    Morocco uses the Africa/Casablanca time zone. Morocco’s time system is slightly different from many European and North American countries because its UTC offset can change at specific times of the year.

    Travelers should therefore check the current Morocco time before flights, airport transfers, international phone calls, online meetings, and time-sensitive tour bookings.

    What Time Is It Now in Rabat, Morocco?

    Travelers frequently ask what time is it now in Rabat Morocco. Rabat is Morocco’s capital and follows exactly the same local time as Casablanca and Marrakech.

    For example, if it is 3:00 PM in Casablanca, it is also 3:00 PM in Rabat. There is no time-zone difference between these Moroccan cities.

    Is Casablanca the Same Time as Marrakech?

    Yes. Casablanca and Marrakech have the same local time. You do not need to change your watch when traveling between the two cities.

    The same applies to Rabat, Fes, Tangier, Agadir, Essaouira, Merzouga, Ouarzazate, and other destinations throughout Morocco.

    Does Morocco Change Its Clocks?

    Morocco has used different daylight-saving arrangements over the years. Because of these changes, travelers searching for the Morocco current time should use a current time source rather than relying on an old fixed time difference.

    As of August 31, 2026, Morocco is operating at UTC+1. Travelers should always verify the latest Africa/Casablanca offset before a flight, transfer, meeting, or other time-sensitive activity.

    Important for travelers: Morocco’s time-zone rules can change. Always check the current local time before your flight, airport transfer, international meeting, or tour departure.

    Morocco Time During Ramadan

    One reason travelers become confused about Morocco time now is the country’s history of adjusting its UTC offset around Ramadan.

    Old travel articles or time-difference charts may therefore show outdated information. Using the Africa/Casablanca time zone is more reliable than assuming that Morocco permanently remains on one fixed UTC offset.

    Morocco Time vs the United States

    Many searches for what time is it in Morocco Africa right now come from travelers in the United States. The exact time difference depends on your U.S. time zone and seasonal clock changes.

    New York, Chicago, Denver, and Los Angeles all use different local times. Therefore, a statement such as “Morocco is always five hours ahead” may not remain accurate throughout the year.
