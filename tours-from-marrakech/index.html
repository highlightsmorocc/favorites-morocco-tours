    Frequently asked questions

    How many days do I need for a desert tour from Marrakech?

    To reach the big dunes of Merzouga (Erg Chebbi), plan 3 days or more. With only 2 days, the tour to the Zagora desert is the best option.

    Can a tour from Marrakech end in Fes?

    Yes. Our 3, 4 and 5 day tours from Marrakech to Fes cross the Sahara and finish in Fes, so you don’t drive back.

    Are the tours from Marrakech private?

    Yes, every tour is private: only your group, your own driver and a flexible itinerary. You confirm with a 20% deposit and cancel free up to 14 days before departure.
