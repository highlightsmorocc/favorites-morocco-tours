    Essential Morocco: 6-Day Private Tour from Casablanca

    A 6-day private tour starting in Casablanca: the Hassan II Mosque, a day in Marrakech, Ait Ben Haddou, a night in the Sahara and an arrival in Fes.

    • 100% private tour
    • Pick-up in Casablanca
    • Sahara camp night
    • Free cancellation up to 14 days
    Tour overview

    From the Atlantic coast to the Sahara and Fes

    Six days is the shortest comfortable way to combine Casablanca, Marrakech, the desert and Fes. The route runs in one direction, so you never drive back over the same road.

    Quick answer

    This tour starts in Casablanca, goes south to Marrakech, crosses the Atlas to Merzouga and ends in Fes. If you need to return to Casablanca for your flight, the final transfer from Fes takes about 3.5 to 4 hours.

    1. Casablanca
    2. Marrakech
    3. Ait Ben Haddou
    4. Dades Valley
    5. Merzouga
    6. Fes
    Day by day

    6-day itinerary step by step

    1

    Casablanca – Hassan II Mosque – Marrakech≈ 240 km · 3 h driving

    Pick-up in Casablanca. Visit the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest in the world, then drive to Marrakech.

    • Night: hotel or riad in Marrakech
    2

    Marrakech – free dayMedina and gardens

    Spend the day in Marrakech: Jemaa el-Fna, the souks, the Bahia Palace and the Majorelle area. An optional local guide can be arranged.

    • Night: hotel or riad in Marrakech
    3

    Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou – Dades Valley≈ 330 km · 8 h with stops

    Cross the Tizi n’Tichka pass, visit Ait Ben Haddou and continue through Ouarzazate to the Dades Valley.

    • Night: hotel in the Dades Valley
    • Dinner included
    4

    Dades – Todra Gorge – Merzouga≈ 300 km · 6–7 h driving

    Walk in Todra Gorge and drive to Merzouga. Camel ride at sunset and a night in a desert camp.

    • Night: desert camp in Merzouga
    • Camel trek included
    5

    Merzouga – Ziz Valley – Midelt – Ifrane – Fes≈ 460 km · 8–9 h driving

    Drive north across the Middle Atlas through Midelt, Azrou and Ifrane to Fes.

    • Night: hotel or riad in Fes
    6

    Fes – medina and departureMorning visit, optional transfer

    Optional guided visit of the medina in the morning. Tour ends in Fes, or we drive you to Casablanca (about 3.5 to 4 hours).

    • End: Fes or Casablanca
    • Optional: local guide

    FAQ

    Questions about the 6 days tour from casablanca

    Where does the tour start and end?

    It starts in Casablanca (airport or hotel) and ends in Fes. A transfer back to Casablanca on the last day is available on request.

    Is the Hassan II Mosque visit included?

    The tour stops at the mosque. The guided interior visit has an entrance fee and a schedule, which we will confirm when you book.

    Can I spend more time in Marrakech?

    Yes. Ask us for a 7-day version, or add an extra night in Marrakech or the desert.

    How do I book the tour?

    Send us your dates by WhatsApp or the contact form. You confirm with a 20% deposit and pay the rest in Morocco. Cancellation is free up to 14 days before departure.
