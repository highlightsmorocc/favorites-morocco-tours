    Morocco Tours from Spain: Ferry and Private Tours

    Spain is only a short ferry ride from Morocco. This guide shows how to cross and start a private tour from Tangier.

    Favorites Morocco Tours · Updated: 27 November 2026

    Quick answer

    You can reach Morocco from Spain by ferry in a few hours, crossing from the south of Spain to Tangier. Many travellers then start a private tour from Tangier with a driver and local guide, on routes of 6 to 15 days across the country. Bring your passport, check the ferry timetable and tell us your arrival time so we can meet you.

    Morocco tours from Spain: frequently asked questions

    How do I get from Spain to Morocco?

    By ferry from southern Spain to Tangier, or by flight to a Moroccan city.

    Can I start a tour at the port?

    Yes. Tell us your arrival time and we plan the meeting.

    Which tours start from Tangier?

    Private tours of 6 to 15 days across Morocco.

    Do I need a passport?

    Yes. Check the entry rules for your nationality before you travel.

    Can I take my car on the ferry?

    Some ferries carry cars, but a private driver is often simpler for a tour.
