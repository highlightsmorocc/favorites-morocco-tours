    When to go

    Best time to visit Morocco

    Morocco is a year-round destination, but each season offers a different experience.

    MAR – MAY

    Spring

    Pleasant temperatures, green valleys and flowering almond trees. The ideal season for the Sahara and the imperial cities.

    ★★★★★ Best season
    JUN – AUG

    Summer

    Very hot inland and in the desert. Perfect for the Atlantic coast and the cooler Atlas Mountains.

    ★★★ Coast & mountains
    SEP – NOV

    Autumn

    Warm days, fewer crowds and the date harvest in the oases. An excellent time for desert tours.

    ★★★★★ Best season
    DEC – FEB

    Winter

    Sunny days but cold desert nights, and snow on the High Atlas peaks. Great for photography.

    ★★★★ Sunny & quiet

    Travel tips

    10 tips before travelling to Morocco

    Practical advice from our local driver-guides to enjoy Morocco with confidence.

    1. Dress modestly. Light clothes covering shoulders and knees are best in medinas and villages.
    2. Carry dirhams in cash. Cards work in hotels, but souks and small shops prefer cash. ATMs are easy to find.
    3. Bargain with a smile. Haggling is part of the fun in the souks — agree on a price before buying.
    4. Ask before taking photos of people, especially in villages and markets.
    5. Drink bottled water and enjoy fresh food from busy, popular places.
    6. Visit the Hassan II Mosque. Most mosques are closed to non-Muslims, but this one in Casablanca offers guided visits.
    7. Respect Ramadan. Avoid eating or drinking in public during the day out of respect.
    8. Pack layers for the desert. Days are warm but nights in the Sahara can be very cold, especially in winter.
    9. Learn a few words. “Salam” (hello) and “Shukran” (thank you) open many smiles.
    10. Check your visa requirements for your nationality before you travel.

    FAQ

    Morocco travel questions

    When is the best time to visit Morocco?

    Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the best times to visit Morocco, with pleasant temperatures for both the imperial cities and the Sahara desert.

    How many days do you need in Morocco?

    For a first trip, 7 to 10 days lets you combine Marrakech, the Atlas Mountains, the Sahara desert and Fes. With 3 or 4 days you can do a desert tour from Marrakech.

    Is Morocco safe for tourists?

    Morocco is one of the most visited countries in Africa and is generally considered safe for travellers. As anywhere, take usual precautions in crowded medinas. Travelling with a private local driver-guide makes everything easier.

    What currency is used in Morocco?

    The Moroccan dirham (MAD). It is a closed currency, so you get it on arrival at exchange offices or ATMs. Cash is needed in souks and small shops.

    What should I wear in Morocco?

    Light, comfortable clothes that cover shoulders and knees, a scarf, good walking shoes and warm layers for desert nights.
