    Volubilis, Moulay Idriss & Meknes Private Day Trip from Fes

    A private full-day trip from Fes to Volubilis, the best-preserved Roman city in Morocco and a UNESCO World Heritage site, with the holy town of Moulay Idriss and the imperial city of Meknes on the same day.

    • 100% private day trip
    • Hotel or riad pick-up in Fes
    • 3 sites in one day
    • Free cancellation up to 14 days
    Trip overview

    Roman ruins and an imperial city, in one day

    Volubilis was a Roman city at the edge of the empire. Today you walk among its arches, columns and floor mosaics, with the green Zerhoun hills all around. Moulay Idriss and Meknes complete the day.

    Quick answer

    Volubilis is about 85 km from Fes, around 1 h 30 by car. The classic day trip combines Volubilis, a stop at Moulay Idriss and a visit of Meknes, then returns to Fes in the late afternoon. Spring and autumn are the best seasons; there is little shade at the ruins.

    Day plan

    Volubilis day trip day plan

    1

    Fes – Volubilis – Moulay Idriss – Meknes – Fes≈ 200 km round trip · 8–9 h

    Pick-up at 8:30 at your hotel or riad in Fes. Drive through olive country to Volubilis and explore the Roman city: the triumphal arch, the basilica, the capitol and the mosaics of the House of Orpheus. Short stop at Moulay Idriss, the holy town on two hills, for the view from the terraces. Continue to Meknes, the city of Sultan Moulay Ismail: Bab Mansour gate, Place El Hedim and the old medina, with time for lunch. Return to Fes in the late afternoon.

    • Entrance fee at Volubilis not included
    • Local guide at the site optional
    • End: your hotel or riad in Fes

    FAQ

    Questions about the Volubilis day trip from Fes

    How far is Volubilis from Fes?

    About 85 km, around 1 h 30 by car. With Moulay Idriss and Meknes the full loop is about 200 km and the day lasts 8 to 9 hours.

    Can I visit Volubilis and Meknes on the same day?

    Yes. It is the classic plan: Volubilis in the morning, Moulay Idriss on the way, and Meknes in the afternoon before driving back to Fes.

    Do I need a guide at Volubilis?

    It is optional. Licensed local guides wait at the entrance and help you understand the mosaics and the history of the Roman city. Your driver can arrange one.

    When is the best time to go?

    Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November). In summer, start early: the site has very little shade. Bring a hat, sunscreen and water.
