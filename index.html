    FAQ

    Questions travellers ask us

    How do I book a tour with Favorites Morocco Tours?

    Send us a message on WhatsApp or by email with your dates and the tour you like. We confirm the itinerary and you pay a 20% deposit; the rest is paid in Morocco.

    Can I cancel my tour?

    Yes. Cancellation is free up to 14 days before departure.

    Are the tours private?

    Yes, all our multi-day tours are private: only your group, with your own driver.

    What is the best tour to see the Sahara from Marrakech?

    For a first trip, the 3 or 4 days tour to Merzouga (Erg Chebbi) is the most popular. With less time, the 2 days tour to Zagora is a good option.

    Do you offer group discounts?

    Yes, groups of 16 travellers or more get 30% off.

    Do your drivers speak English and Spanish?

    Yes, we work with English and Spanish speaking drivers and guides.
