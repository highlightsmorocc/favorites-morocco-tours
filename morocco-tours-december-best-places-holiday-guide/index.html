    Morocco Tours December:
    Best Places & Holiday Guide

    Mild sunny days, magical Sahara nights, festive Christmas & New Year — discover why December is one of the smartest times to explore Morocco.

    Updated for the 2025–2026 season · By Favorites Morocco Tours

    Morocco Tours December offer the perfect balance of comfortable weather, fewer crowds (outside the holiday peak), and authentic experiences. From the golden dunes of the Sahara to the lively medinas of Marrakech and the imperial cities, December delivers unforgettable moments.

    This complete guide covers weather by region, the best places to visit, Christmas & New Year celebrations, recommended itineraries, packing tips, and how to book private tours from Marrakech or Casablanca with Favorites Morocco Tours.

    Why Choose Morocco Tours in December?

    December sits in Morocco’s winter season, yet the country remains surprisingly inviting. Daytime temperatures in Marrakech, the southern valleys and the Sahara regularly reach 18–22°C, while Agadir offers some of the best winter sun in North Africa.

    • Comfortable sightseeing — no extreme summer heat
    • Fewer crowds outside Christmas–New Year week
    • Excellent value on riads and hotels
    • Spectacular winter light perfect for photography
    • Ideal desert conditions — warm days & starry cold nights
    • Festive atmosphere without commercial overload

    Best Places to Visit on Morocco Tours December

    1. Marrakech – The Perfect December Base

    Marrakech shines in December with mild days perfect for exploring the medina, souks, Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs and Majorelle Garden. Evenings are cool and atmospheric. It is also the ideal starting point for tours from Marrakech to the Sahara and Atlas Mountains.

    2. The Sahara Desert – Merzouga & Erg Chebbi

    December is one of the best months for the Sahara. Comfortable daytime temperatures make camel treks and dune walks a pleasure, while nights are cold and crystal-clear — perfect for stargazing. Many travellers choose to spend New Year’s Eve in a desert camp under the stars.

    3. High Atlas Mountains & Berber Villages

    Snow-capped peaks create dramatic scenery. Lower valleys (Ourika, Imlil) remain accessible for day hikes and authentic Berber village experiences.

    4. Casablanca & the Atlantic Coast

    Casablanca is the main international gateway. Visit the Hassan II Mosque and continue on to Rabat, Fes or the south. Explore our tours from Casablanca.

    5. Fes – Cultural Deep Dive

    The ancient medina feels more intimate in winter. Cooler temperatures make walking the 9,000 alleys of Fes el-Bali more comfortable.

    6. Essaouira & Agadir

    Essaouira offers relaxed coastal charm, while Agadir provides the warmest winter sun on the Atlantic coast — ideal for ending a tour.

    Christmas & New Year in Morocco

    Christmas Day is a normal working day in Morocco — everything stays open. Many riads and hotels decorate and offer special menus. New Year’s Eve is lively, especially in Marrakech (rooftop parties, gala dinners) and in luxury desert camps.

    Booking tip: For 24 December – 2 January, reserve riads and desert camps 2–3 months in advance. Outside this peak, last-minute tours Morocco December are often available.

    Recommended Morocco Tours December Itineraries

    7-Day Classic: Marrakech + Sahara

    1. Arrive Marrakech – medina & Jemaa el-Fna
    2. Full day Marrakech (palaces, souks, hammam)
    3. High Atlas → Aït Benhaddou → Dades Valley
    4. Todra Gorge → Merzouga – camel trek & desert camp
    5. Sunrise in the dunes – return journey
    6. Back to Marrakech
    7. Free morning & departure

    10-Day Imperial Cities + Sahara

    Casablanca → Rabat → Fes → Middle Atlas → Merzouga Desert → Dades/Todra → Aït Benhaddou → Marrakech. Perfect for travellers starting from Casablanca.

    What to Pack for December

    • Light layers for daytime + warm jacket for evenings
    • Comfortable closed walking shoes
    • Scarf / shawl
    • Sunglasses & sunscreen
    • Warm sleepwear for desert camps
    • Modest clothing for religious sites

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is December a good time to visit Morocco? Yes. Mild temperatures in the south and centre, excellent desert conditions, and a relaxed festive atmosphere make it one of the best months.
    Is the Sahara good in December? Excellent. Warm days and clear, cold nights create ideal conditions for camel treks and stargazing.
    Should I book early for Morocco tours December 2025? Yes for Christmas & New Year. For the rest of the month, 4–8 weeks is usually enough.
