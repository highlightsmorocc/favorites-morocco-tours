    Things to Do in Marrakech: 15 Must-See Sights and Experiences

    Marrakech mixes palaces, gardens, souks and one of the liveliest squares in the world. Here are the sights and experiences we recommend to our travellers, with tips to enjoy them at the right time.

    Favorites Morocco Tours · Updated: 26 November 2026

    Quick answer

    With two days in Marrakech, see Jemaa el-Fna at dusk, the souks, the Bahia Palace, the Koutoubia, the Saadian Tombs, Ben Youssef Madrasa and Jardin Majorelle, then add a hammam and a rooftop dinner. With more time, take a day trip to the Atlas mountains, Agafay, Ouzoud or Essaouira.

    The medina: squares, souks and monuments

    Start at Jemaa el-Fna, the main square, quiet in the morning and transformed at sunset into an open-air theatre of food stalls, musicians and storytellers. Behind it, the souks are organised by craft: leather, lanterns, spices, carpets. The Koutoubia minaret, the symbol of the city, stands next to the square.

    Do not miss the Bahia Palace and its painted ceilings, the Saadian Tombs, the El Badi Palace ruins and the restored Ben Youssef Madrasa with its carved cedar and stucco.

    Gardens and quiet corners

    Jardin Majorelle, with its cobalt-blue villa and cactus garden, is next to the Yves Saint Laurent Museum; arrive at opening time to avoid queues. Le Jardin Secret in the medina and the Menara olive grove are calmer alternatives.

    Experiences to try

    Book a traditional hammam with a black-soap scrub, take a cooking class to learn tajine and couscous, and have dinner on a rooftop with a view of the Atlas at sunset. In the evening, the new town of Gueliz has modern restaurants and cafés.

    Easy day trips from Marrakech

    The Agafay desert is 45 minutes away for quad bikes, camels and sunset dinners. The Ourika Valley and Imlil offer Berber villages and walks in the High Atlas. The Ouzoud Waterfalls, Ait Ben Haddou and Essaouira on the coast each make a full and rewarding day.

    Practical tips

    Agree on taxi prices before getting in, or ask your riad to book a driver. Bargaining is normal in the souks: smile, take your time and only make an offer if you want to buy. Dress respectfully in the medina and keep small change for tips.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many days do you need in Marrakech?

    Two full days for the main sights, three or four with a day trip to the Atlas, Agafay or Essaouira.

    Is Marrakech safe for tourists?

    Yes, Marrakech is generally safe. Watch your belongings in crowded places, ignore unofficial guides and use registered taxis or a private driver.

    What is the best area to stay in Marrakech?

    A riad in the medina for atmosphere, or Gueliz and Hivernage for modern hotels and quieter evenings.
