    Frequently asked questions

    Can I start a Morocco tour from the Tangier ferry port?

    Yes, your private driver can meet you at the Tangier Med or Tangier Ville port, at the airport or at your hotel.

    What is the best tour from Tangier?

    The 7 and 10 day tours from Tangier are the most complete for a first visit: Chefchaouen, Fes, the Sahara and Marrakech.

    How long is the drive from Tangier to Chefchaouen?

    About 2 to 3 hours, which makes Chefchaouen the ideal first stop.
