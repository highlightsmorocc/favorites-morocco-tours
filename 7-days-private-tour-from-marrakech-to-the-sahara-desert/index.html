    The Grand Sahara Loop: 7-Day Private Tour from Marrakech

    A 7-day private tour from Marrakech to the Sahara Desert with your own driver: cross the High Atlas, sleep in a kasbah at Ait Ben Haddou, drive the Dades and Todra gorges, ride a camel into the Merzouga dunes and come back through the palm groves of the Draa Valley.

    • 100% private tour
    • Hotel pick-up in Marrakech
    • 2 nights in the Sahara
    • Free cancellation up to 14 days
    Tour overview

    Morocco in one week: mountains, kasbahs and the Sahara

    This 7 days tour from Marrakech is our most complete desert itinerary. It is a private loop, so you travel only with your family or friends, stop wherever you want and sleep in hand-picked kasbahs, riads and a luxury camp in the Erg Chebbi dunes.

    Quick answer

    The best 7-day tour from Marrakech is a loop through Ait Ben Haddou, the Dades and Todra gorges and the Merzouga dunes, returning via the Draa Valley and Telouet. Seven days give you two nights in the Sahara and only one long driving day, so the trip feels relaxed, not rushed.

    Day by day

    7-day Morocco itinerary from Marrakech

    1

    Marrakech – Tizi n’Tichka – Ait Ben Haddou≈ 190 km · 4 h driving

    Pick-up at your riad or hotel in Marrakech at 8:30. Cross the High Atlas on the Tizi n’Tichka pass (2,260 m), with photo stops in Berber villages and mint tea with a local family. In the afternoon, explore the UNESCO-listed ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, the film set of Gladiator and Game of Thrones, and watch the sunset from the old granary at the top of the hill.

    • Night: kasbah in Ait Ben Haddou
    • Dinner included
    2

    Ouarzazate – Skoura – Valley of Roses – Dades Gorge≈ 170 km · 3.5 h driving

    Drive through Ouarzazate, “the door of the desert”, to the palm grove of Skoura and Kasbah Amridil. Follow the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs to Kelaat M’Gouna, the home of Moroccan rose water, then climb the famous hairpin bends of the Dades Gorge and the “Monkey Fingers” rock formations.

    • Night: hotel in the Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
    3

    Todra Gorge – Erfoud – Merzouga camel trek≈ 290 km · 5 h driving

    Walk between the rock walls of the Todra Gorge, up to 300 metres high, near Tinghir. Continue via the fossil workshops of Erfoud to Merzouga. At sunset, ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes to your private desert camp: a tent with en-suite bathroom, dinner under the stars and Berber drums around the fire.

    • Night: luxury desert camp
    • Camel trek included
    • Breakfast & dinner
    4

    A full day in the Sahara DesertMerzouga · Erg Chebbi

    Wake up early for the sunrise from the top of the dunes. Then explore the desert by 4x4: the Gnawa village of Khamlia and its music, the Rissani market on the old Sijilmasa caravan route, nomad families and the seasonal lake of Dayet Srji. The afternoon is free for sandboarding or an optional quad ride.

    • Night: desert camp or hotel with pool
    • 4x4 desert tour included
    • Breakfast & dinner
    5

    Merzouga – Alnif – N’Kob – Draa Valley≈ 360 km · 6.5 h driving

    The longest stage of the tour, broken up with plenty of stops: the fossil village of Alnif, the Saghro mountains and N’Kob, the village of the 45 kasbahs. In the late afternoon you reach the Draa Valley, the palm groves of Morocco’s longest river, and your kasbah in Agdz.

    • Night: kasbah in the Draa Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
    6

    Agdz – Ouarzazate – Ounila Valley – Telouet≈ 150 km · 3 h driving

    Visit Kasbah Tamnougalt, then continue to Ouarzazate and the Atlas Film Studios (optional entry). In the afternoon, follow the Ounila Valley, the old caravan road over the Atlas, to Telouet and the painted salons of the Glaoui kasbah.

    • Night: guesthouse in Telouet
    • Breakfast & dinner
    7

    Telouet – Tizi n’Tichka – Marrakech≈ 170 km · 3.5 h driving

    Cross the High Atlas one last time, stop at a women’s argan oil cooperative and enjoy lunch with mountain views. Drop-off at your accommodation in Marrakech in the early afternoon, with time left to enjoy Jemaa el-Fna at night.

    • End: your hotel in Marrakech
    • Breakfast included

    FAQ

    Questions about the 7 days tour from Marrakech

    Is 7 days enough to see Morocco from Marrakech?

    Yes. Seven days is the ideal length for a desert tour from Marrakech: you see the High Atlas, Ait Ben Haddou, the Dades and Todra gorges, two nights in the Sahara and the Draa Valley without long, tiring driving days.

    Is this 7-day tour private?

    Yes, it is 100% private. The car, the driver and the schedule are only for you, from one traveller to large families, and you can change the stops or the hotels at any time.

    When is the best time for a Sahara tour from Marrakech?

    March to May and September to November are the best months, with warm days and cool nights. Summer is very hot in the desert (often above 40 °C) and winter nights in Merzouga can drop close to 0 °C.

    Can the tour end in Fes instead of Marrakech?

    Yes. From Merzouga we can drive north through Midelt and Ifrane and finish the tour in Fes. Just tell us when you request your quote.

    Is this tour good for families with children?

    Yes. The private format lets you take breaks whenever you need them, and the camel trek, the dunes and the desert camp are favourites with children. Child seats are available on request.

    How do I book the tour?

    Send us your dates by WhatsApp or the contact form. You confirm with a 20% deposit and pay the rest in Morocco. Cancellation is free up to 14 days before departure.
